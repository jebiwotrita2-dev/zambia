Direct to Cell connects phones to satellites.

The Starlink network is designed to work like a cell tower in space. With an authorized mobile partner, a compatible LTE phone can access satellite data where terrestrial coverage is unavailable.

Compatibility, coverage, and available services may vary by country and rollout.
